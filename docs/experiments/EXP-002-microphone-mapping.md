# EXP-002 — Internal far-field microphone mapping

**Status:** PLANNED · **Risk:** consented short audio capture

**Question:** Which source, if any, contains the built-in room microphone signal independently of the Magic Remote?

**Prerequisites:** EXP-001 completed; no third-party speech present; explicit approval to record. Inventory capture devices and capabilities first. Capture only brief, explicitly consented test speech from each candidate device; identify channels and formats, clipping and latency. Do not kill or replace the LG capture service.

**Acceptance:** exact TV/firmware/source with reproducible mic PCM, or a bounded negative finding. Remove temporary audio files and publish only sanitized metadata.
