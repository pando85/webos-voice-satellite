# EXP-006 — Echo reference and AEC feasibility

**Status:** IN PROGRESS: on c3-a (P2 session 1, 2026-11-02) `hw:LGSIC,1` SpeakerFeedback was classified **unusable as a linear AEC reference** (coherence with the mic echo 0.5 to 0.6, linear ceiling 3 to 7 dB in 200 to 4000 Hz, spurious content 7 dB below a pure tone), although it is linked with the mic, 1.4 ms ahead and drift-free. `hw:LGSIC,3` SoundEngineCapture is clean, post-volume, drift-free and reached oracle-level ERLE, but its delay changes per open (12.9 to 19.1 ms): the candidate reference. Open: the AEC evaluation proper, delay behaviour over long sessions, other outputs. See [P2 session 1](../discovery/c3-a/P2-session1-audible-and-echo.md#2-d4-echo-and-the-speakerfeedback-reference-exp-006). The canceller (sonora AEC3 with the SoundEngineCapture reference) is implemented in the daemon, off by default, with host-only results on the session 1 recordings and an on-TV plan: [AEC front-end](../design/aec-frontend.md) · **Risk:** controlled audio capture/output with approval Session 2 recorded mic + SoundEngineCapture sets with TV content at volume 10 and 15 (delay 18.7 and 15.0 ms per open; coherence with a summed reference 0.93 for mono speech but 0.39 to 0.81 for stereo content) ([P2 session 2](../discovery/c3-a/P2-session2-wake-accuracy.md#4-aec-recording-set-and-offline-baseline)).

**Question:** Can the TV provide a sample-aligned reference of what it actually emits, and can we use existing LG DSP output?

**Prerequisites:** EXP-002/003 and EXP-005. Investigate audio routing and loopbacks read-only; do not call every ALSA capture device a microphone or loopback. Compare output path including HDMI/eARC, buffering and latency; only then evaluate external AEC.

**Acceptance:** documented signal provenance and timing/quality measurements. An AEC implementation is NOT planned without a reference and a baseline comparison.
