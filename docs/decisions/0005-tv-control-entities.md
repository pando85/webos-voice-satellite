# ADR 0005: TV control from Home Assistant through separate satellite entities

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-11-04, owner). **Decision 4 amended on 2026-11-09 (owner)**: Home Assistant's "turn off the TV" is a real power-off (the remote's power key) when the satellite keeps running afterwards; see [Amendment 2026-11-09](#amendment-2026-11-09-decision-4-a-real-power-off-from-ha). **Decision 3 amended by [ADR 0007](0007-tv-media-player.md)** (same day, owner): a control-only TV `media_player` is added next to the separate entities, so HA's built-in volume, mute and turn on/off intents reach the TV. **Note (2026-11-05, [ADR 0008](0008-user-initiated-lg-settings.md)):** the "no TV settings writes" limit of the design is relaxed for Always Ready and LG hands-free only, written by the TV app on a user's press, never by the daemon or these entities.
- **Scope:** whether and how the satellite exposes TV controls (power, screen, volume, mute, input, apps) to Home Assistant. Design: [TV control](../design/tv-control.md). Not implemented; setters untested ([EXP-010](../experiments/EXP-010-tv-control-setters.md)).
- **Related:** [ADR 0001](0001-esphome-native-api.md), [backlog #11](../project/backlog.md#11-tv-control-from-home-assistant-via-the-satellite), [always-on design](../design/always-on.md)

## Context

- The satellite runs as root on the TV and already calls luna services with `luna-send`: the power-state subscription, `tvpower/power/powerOn {"reason":"remoteKey"}`, `networkinput/controls/power` and `tvpower/power/turnOffScreen` ([always-on section 13](../design/always-on.md#13-implemented-in-the-daemon-010-and-checked-on-c3-b)).
- On c3-b (firmware `03.30.73`, 2026-11-04) the getters for volume and mute, sound output, external inputs, foreground app, media play state and launch points answered as root with no SSAP pairing (`verified-on-target` for that unit only). The setters are declared but untested; `turnOnScreen` and the power path are verified on c3-b ([D5](../discovery/c3-b/D5-results.md), [D6](../discovery/c3-b/D6-poweroff-screenoff.md)). c3-a (`03.30.74`) is untested.
- A real power-off either suspends the TV (the daemon is frozen) or leaves it in `Active Standby` (replies not audible). In `Screen Off` the satellite listens and replies are audible (verified on c3-b).
- HA's `webostv` integration needs SSAP pairing and documents Wake-on-LAN for turning on. The owner's HA has no `webostv` entry.
- The satellite's ESPHome `media_player` already means "reply volume".

## Decision

1. **Complement HA's `webostv` integration, do not replace it.** The satellite adds no-pairing, standby-aware and voice-friendly control; owners may run both.
2. **v1 controls:** power and screen, TV volume and mute, input selection, app launch. **No toasts or notifications in v1.**
3. **Separate entities** (`switch`, `number`, `select`, `button`, sensors), not a TV `media_player`. The existing media player stays the reply volume. *(Amended 2026-11-04 by [ADR 0007](0007-tv-media-player.md): the separate entities and the reply-volume player stay, and a second, control-only media player "TV" is added; HA's Spanish volume and mute intents only target media players, [P4](../discovery/c3-b/P4-sound-session.md).)*
4. **Power-off only into a state where the satellite keeps running, only when enabled.** An HA power-off sends the remote's power key and relies on the always-on mode "Keep listening (screen off)" (wake and darken) to end in `Screen Off`. It is opt-in (`[tv_control] power_off`, default off), not listed when off, and refused unless the always-on mode is selected and the darkener would act. No real Suspend power-off from HA. *(Amended 2026-11-09, owner, [below](#amendment-2026-11-09-decision-4-a-real-power-off-from-ha): the TV media player's turn off sends the power key whenever the satellite keeps running afterwards, without `power_off`: in "Turn off normally" with Always Ready on it ends in LG's `Active Standby`, in "Keep listening" in `Screen Off` as before; with Always Ready off or unknown it stays a screen off. Still no power-off into `Suspend`.)*
5. **Listening indicator as HA entities only:** truthful `listening` and `microphone_muted` binary sensors tied to the real capture PCM state. No on-TV indicator.

Also decided by the owner on 2026-11-04: the audible timer and announcement check is skipped for now; setters are tested on c3-b only when the owner says so; the conversation agent for the Kiwi pipeline is deferred ([backlog #12](../project/backlog.md#12-conversation-agent-for-the-kiwi-pipeline)).

## Amendment 2026-11-09: decision 4, a real power-off from HA

- **Owner decision (2026-11-09):** decision 4 is outdated. HA's "turn off the TV" must be a real power-off, as the remote's power key, because the satellite is verified to keep working in `Active Standby` with Always Ready on. Owner report that led to it: with the default config (`remote_off` normal, no `[tv]` or `power_off` setting) every HA turn off was a `turnOffScreen`, so the TV stayed fully on in `Screen Off` (app sound kept playing, red standby LED off). Observation from the owner-authorised read-only read of c3-b's daemon log on 2026-11-09: three HA turn offs ended in `Screen Off` stays of 41, 45 and 99 min (no raw log content in the repository).
- **Evidence:**
  - `verified-on-target` (c3-b, firmware `03.30.73`, 2026-11-03, D5 R1b, R3 and R4): after the API power key with Always Ready on (`alwaysReady`, and `allEnabled` in R4) the TV stays in `Active Standby`; the daemon, the HA link and the microphone PCM stayed up for 59 min 18 s (one pid, 702 of 703 samples `RUNNING`, 0 ping losses) and six wake word detections ran the full chain (HA wake word, Whisper transcript, intent, TTS streamed to the satellite) ([D5 results](../discovery/c3-b/D5-results.md#answer), [Always Ready soak](../discovery/c3-b/D5-results.md#always-ready-soak-r3-api-key-alwaysready)).
  - `verified-on-target` (installed package, self-check in `Active Standby`): 0.6.1 on c3-b (`03.30.73`, 2026-11-08: HA connected 1.4 s after the start, self-check passed, no WARN or ERROR; [c3-b P4](../discovery/c3-b/P4-permanent-install.md#update-to-061-2026-11-08-key-grab-started)) and 0.6.0 on c3-a (`03.30.74`, 2026-11-07: self-check passed, no WARN or ERROR, `pcm0c`/`pcm3c` `RUNNING` in `Active Standby`; [c3-a P4](../discovery/c3-a/P4-permanent-install.md#update-to-060-2026-11-07-remote-key-grab-off)). c3-a was not entered into `Active Standby` by the satellite's key.
  - `verified-on-target` (c3-b, D5 R1a and R5): with Always Ready **off** the same key ends in `Suspend` 7 to 15 s later: network down, the daemon frozen ([D5 results](../discovery/c3-b/D5-results.md#answer)). This is what the Always Ready guard below avoids.
- **Decision (replaces the opt-in rule of decision 4 for the TV media player):**
  1. "Turn off normally" (`remote_off = normal`): the TV media player's turn off from a lit TV sends the remote's power key (`networkinput/controls/power`) when Always Ready is on (`lifeOnScreenMode` `alwaysReady` or `allEnabled`). The setting is **read again right before the key** with the same read-only getter as the `lg_always_ready` sensor of [ADR 0008](0008-user-initiated-lg-settings.md) (`settingsservice/getSystemSettings`, no new endpoint), and the key is decided on that fresh value; the reply also updates the sensor, as the 60 s poll does. A read that fails or does not answer within 2 s counts as unknown. The TV goes to `Active Standby`, as with the remote. With Always Ready off, unknown (sensor off, read failed) or an unrecognised value, it falls back to `turnOffScreen` and logs why at `info`.
  2. "Keep listening (screen off)": unchanged in effect (power key, then wake and darken to `Screen Off`), but no longer needs `power_off = true`, so HA's turn off is what the remote's power key does in both modes. Always Ready is not checked there: the darkener wakes the TV before LG's suspend (verified with Always Ready off, c3-b, D6 and EXP-010 B7).
  3. Preconditions kept for the key: the daemon's view known, setters not blocked by the firmware guard, the power state known and settled `Active` or `Screen Saver` (the key is a toggle), the kill switch absent, one key at a time: after a key, further power commands (the media player's turn on and off, `tv_power` on and off) are refused until the TV leaves the lit power class it was sent from (a transition, `Screen Off` or standby; `Active` to `Screen Saver` does not count), or for 10 s (the luna reply comes before the state changes, and a second key in that gap would turn the TV back on; the key reaches `Active Standby` in about 3 to 4 s, D5); in "Keep listening" also the darkener's cooldown and hourly budget; in "Turn off normally" the key counts against TV control's global 30 per minute. A refusal other than Always Ready stays a refusal (no fallback to the screen off). Already `Screen Off` or `Active Standby`: nothing, as before.
  4. The `tv_power` switch stays opt-in (`power_off`) with the same object id; off is the same power key with the same preconditions, fresh read and routes, with no screen-off fallback (that is `tv_screen`); on is the media player's turn on (not gated by Always Ready or the kill switch); its name is now "TV power". `tv_screen` keeps meaning `turnOffScreen`.
- **Known limitation that stays:** replies, announcements and music are **not audible in `Active Standby`** (D5 table rows R1b and R4, the DSP input gain near zero in [D6 variant C](../discovery/c3-b/D6-poweroff-screenoff.md#variant-c-why-active-standby-is-silent), cell P2 of the [voice and power screen](../design/voice-and-power-screen.md)). This is already the case after the remote's power key in "Turn off normally" today. A reply or music that plays when the turn off arrives is cut by the standby (open question, no delay mechanism built).
- **Residual unknowns:**
  - HA turn on from a settled `Active Standby` (`powerOn` after the key block) is unit-tested only; EXP-010 B11 is PARTIAL (transient `Active Standby` only). The `powerOn` path itself is verified from settled `Active Standby` on c3-b (D6).
  - c3-a: the TV power paths (the key, `Active Standby` entered by the satellite, the way back) are NOT TESTED.
  - The new behaviour is NOT TESTED on either TV: [EXP-010 Part C](../experiments/EXP-010-tv-control-setters.md#part-c-ha-turn-off-as-a-real-power-off-adr-0005-decision-4-amended-2026-11-09) is the procedure (owner authorization needed).
  - Always Ready off path: the fresh read closes the 60 s staleness of the sensor's poll (review, 2026-11-09). What remains: whether `lifeOnScreenMode` can change between the read and LG's power-off (a window of under about 2 s plus the key), and whether every value other than `alwaysReady` and `allEnabled` is correctly treated as unsafe on other firmware. With `lg_settings = false` the read is not made and the turn off is always a screen off in "Turn off normally".
  - Whether a satellite-sent key right after a settle hits tvpowerd's 1.5 s key block (seen for `powerOn` in D6) is not known; the key is only sent from a settled lit state.
- **Rollback:** the previous package (0.6.1) restores the old mapping (screen off unless `power_off` and "Keep listening").

## Consequences

- New entities on the satellite device; a new sans-IO crate for TV control, separate from capture, voice and playback; the ESPHome API crate needs generic `switch` and `text_sensor` entities.
- Volume, mute, input and app stay off by default until EXP-010 passes on c3-b; a control whose setter fails there stays off. (Update 2026-11-04: Part A passed for all four on c3-b `03.30.73`; the owner then made them **on by default**, and decided that TV control is **not gated by the firmware guard** (a warning is logged on an unvalidated firmware; see the [design section 9](../design/tv-control.md#9-configuration)).)
- Every control is gated by a startup capability check; a failed method gives an entity with a missing state, never a guessed value.
- An HA "turn off the TV" leaves the TV running with a dark screen: more power than standby (not measured), LG's power-off OLED care may not run, a brief panel flash, and a 5 s second press. These costs are the always-on mode's, shown next to the setting. *(Since the 2026-11-09 amendment this holds in "Keep listening" only; in "Turn off normally" with Always Ready on HA's turn off is LG's own power-off into `Active Standby`, where replies are silent.)*
- Waking a TV from Suspend stays outside the satellite (HA's Wake-on-LAN or the remote).
- Voice for numbers and selects needs HA scripts, custom sentences or an LLM agent; switches work with HA's built-in intents.

## Alternatives considered

- **Replace `webostv`:** would duplicate a maintained integration and its media features; the satellite's advantage is only standby and pairing.
- **One TV `media_player`:** clashes with the reply-volume media player, and its single state mixes power, input and volume. (Revisited in [ADR 0007](0007-tv-media-player.md): added as a second, control-only player with its own key, in addition to the separate entities.)
- **Real power-off (key without the always-on mode, or `tvpower/power/powerOff`):** the satellite is frozen in Suspend or cannot reply in `Active Standby`.
- **Power-off as `turnOffScreen`:** keeps the running app's sound playing; kept as the separate screen switch.
- **Toasts and an on-TV listening indicator:** deferred by the owner; no controllable status LED exists.

## Revisit when

- EXP-010 shows a setter does not work or behaves differently per sound output or power state.
- c3-a or another model or firmware gives different results.
- The owner wants toasts, an on-TV indicator, or a real standby power-off. *(Standby power-off: done by the 2026-11-09 amendment.)*
- EXP-010 Part C fails on c3-b, or the satellite does not keep running in `Active Standby` on c3-a or another firmware.
- HA's `webostv` integration gains standby control without pairing, or the owner adds it and the two conflict.
