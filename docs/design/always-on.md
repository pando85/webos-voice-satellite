# Always-on design: listening and replying while the TV screen is off

- **Status:** **proposal, nothing implemented.** Desk research plus read-only inspection of c3-a on 2026-11-03. No power state was changed, no setting was written, no sound was played. The test procedure is the [D5 plan](../discovery/c3-a/D5-plan.md). **It was run on a second unit, c3-b, on 2026-11-03** ([results](../discovery/c3-b/D5-results.md), summary in [section 11](#11-result-on-c3-b-2026-11-03)); it has not been run on c3-a.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03. Device in scope for TV facts: c3-a (LG C3, webOS TV 8.3.0, firmware 03.30.74, rooted with the Homebrew Channel). Other models and firmware are not covered; a finding from another LG model is not confirmation on this one.
- **Owner question (2026-11-03):** can the satellite run all the time, listen with the TV screen off and still work, and can it play sound with the screen off?
- **Related:** [autostart design](autostart.md), [install and configuration UX](install-and-config-ux.md), [EXP-004](../experiments/EXP-004-power-lifecycle.md), [P3 robustness](../discovery/c3-a/P3-robustness.md), [D2 audio stack](../discovery/c3-a/D2-audio-stack.md), [roadmap Phase 3](../project/roadmap.md#phase-3-lifecycle-and-robustness).

Labels follow [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md): `documented` (vendor or upstream text), `observed-on-other-device` (reports or code for other LG TVs), `hypothesis`, `verified-on-target` (seen on c3-a). "Verified-on-target" below means a read-only observation of the device as it is now (TV on, screen on); it never means a power state was entered.

## 1. Short answer

| Question | Answer today |
| --- | --- |
| Can it run all the time? | **Yes while the TV is on, including with the screen off** (the process, the capture PCM and the HA connection do not depend on the panel; hypothesis until D5). **No while the TV is in standby (Suspend)**: the process is frozen, not killed, and the network is not reliably up (observed-on-other-device). A TV powered off with Quick Start+ off is a cold boot and everything restarts. |
| How to listen with the screen off? | Use **"screen off" on a running TV** (luna `tvpower/power/turnOffScreen`, the HA webostv "Screen" switch, or the OLED Care "Screen Off" menu), not the remote's power button. This is the recommended mode ([section 4](#4-what-always-on-realistically-means-and-recommended-modes)). |
| Can the remote's power key keep it listening with the TV looking off? | **Yes on c3-b with Always Ready on** (`Active Standby`, process, mic and HA link alive for 59 min 18 s, wake word chain ran); **no with Always Ready off** (the TV suspends 7 to 15 s later and the process is frozen). The API key and the real IR remote key gave the same state sequences. Panel darkness and power draw not observed. [Section 11](#11-result-on-c3-b-2026-11-03) |
| Can it play sound with the screen off? | **Yes with `turnOffScreen` (state `Screen Off`)**: a `pacat` tone was heard by the TV's microphone on c3-b. **No in the `Active Standby` state that the remote's power-off gives with Always Ready on**: the tone was silent at the mic in 5 checks, on ARC and on the TV speaker (verified-on-target, c3-b). In `Suspend` nothing runs. |

## 4. What "always on" realistically means and recommended modes

Three facts bound the design:

1. **Our process is frozen in Suspend** and the capture PCM and network are not ours to keep (observed-on-other-device). Nothing in the repository or in public reports gives a way to keep a rooted daemon running through LG's standby. The only LG feature that listens with the TV "off" is LG's own wake-on-voice, which runs in LG's firmware and ends in LG's assistant. We do not claim a way to reuse it.
2. **A TV that is "on" with the panel dark behaves like a running computer** (documented for the screen-off feature). That is the state in which the satellite can listen and speak.
3. **Auto-off timers can undo it.** On c3-a `noSignalAutoOff` and `autoOff15Min` are on (15 minutes without signal turns the TV off), `screenSaverEnabled` is on. If the screen-off TV sits on an HDMI input with no signal, the TV may power itself off into Suspend and freeze the satellite (**hypothesis**; the effect of the timers while `Screen Off` is not known).

**Recommended operating modes, in order**

| Mode | What the user does | Satellite | Trade-offs | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Screen off (recommended)** | Leaves the TV on and turns the screen off with HA (webostv "Screen" switch), the remote shortcut or a future satellite entity; stays on a real app or an input with signal | Runs, listens, replies; HA connection stays up | Panel power saved; SoC, audio and Wi-Fi stay on, so draw is above standby (**no figure measured**, plug meter planned). OLED: the panel is off, which avoids static-image wear, but OLED compensation and pixel refresh runs after power-off (`compensationStart`/`End` reasons exist, [lg-webos-dashboard PR 150](https://github.com/rorygallagher2024/lg-webos-dashboard/pull/150)); a TV that is never "off" may skip it (**hypothesis**). The TV's `Screen Off` may end on a remote key press or an auto-off timer | hypothesis until D5 steps 1, 3 |
| **B. Always Ready on** | Uses the remote power key; the TV shows its background screen | Probably runs (OS awake for the app) and keeps the HA link; may lose the mic to LG's voice daemon (MIC BUSY) | LG warns consumption rises; the background screen lights the panel (burn-in is LG's concern here, screen-off timer `screenOffTime`); `Hi LG` and ours would compete for the PCM | unknown, D5 steps 4 to 5 |
| **C. Standby with wake on demand** | Remote power key; HA turns the TV on by WoL when needed (webostv turn-on), then optionally screen-off | Frozen while in standby, runs again after wake (reconnect, PCM reopen) | Lowest draw; **no voice assistant while in standby**; WoL may not work with Quick Start+ on | works as "off", not as "always on" |
| **D. Cold off, boot on demand** | Quick Start+ off, Always Ready off | Starts from `init.d` at each power-on (60 s delay in the draft hook) | Slow wake (a boot); needs the persistent autostart | fallback |

**Can the satellite request screen-on or a wake when a reply needs it?** For voice replies: no need, audio does not need the panel (hypothesis for mode A, D5 step 3). If a visual or a "wake the TV" feature is wanted later: with the TV running, `turnOnScreen` over luna (declared on c3-a; the HA "Screen" switch uses the same method over the network, documented); from standby the satellite cannot (it is frozen), HA can (WoL, `wakeOnLan`/`wakeOnWiFi` declared). Note that HA's turn-on is itself WoL-based on webOS, so it shares the Wi-Fi/Quick Start+ caveats above.

**Power-draw trade-off.** No measured figure exists for c3-a in any state. LG states that energy consumption "may increase" in Always Ready and in standby-mode features (documented). Mode A should draw less than screen-on and more than Suspend (hypothesis). The D5 plan asks for a plug-meter reading in each state (owner's plug or the homelab power metering if one exists) before this document recommends anything beyond "try it".

## 5. Playback with the screen off: known and to test

- **Known:** the replies reach PulseAudio and the TV speaker with the screen on and in the `ScreenSaver` state (verified-on-target, [P1](../discovery/c3-a/P1-playback-silent-validation.md), [P2 session 1](../discovery/c3-a/P2-session1-audible-and-echo.md)). The screen-off feature is documented to leave sound playing.
- **Not known on c3-a:** whether our `pacat` stream is heard in `Screen Off` (an A/V mute service, `avmutemanager`, runs on the TV and its trigger rules are unknown), whether the master volume and mute are unchanged by the transition, whether the amp/DSP path stays powered, and whether the TV levels the sound the same way. Also unknown: whether playback works in Always Ready, and the latency of the first reply after a long idle period with the screen off.
- **Must be tested (D5):** a short, quiet reply-equivalent tone in each state that is safe to enter, with the TV volume read first and restored to the exact original value ([D5 plan](../discovery/c3-a/D5-plan.md#playback-check-short-and-quiet)).

## 7. Exposing power options in the UI (design)

**Rule (owner, 2026-11-03):** if D5 shows that a power-related setting is **critical** for the satellite to work, it must be exposed as a configuration option, both as an **HA entity on the device page** and in the **install and status app** planned in [install and configuration UX](install-and-config-ux.md) (the web app in the HBC package, section 4.1). Each option carries its risks next to it. Design only; nothing here is implemented, and which options are critical is decided by the D5 evidence.

**Candidate options** (each is added only if D5 shows it matters):

| Option | Values | Becomes critical if | HA entity (config category, one per option) | Revert |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TV screen policy | `leave alone`, `screen off when idle (N min)`, `screen off now` | Mode A is the only state where listening and replies work | `select` plus a `switch` "Screen off" mirroring the real state | The switch, or any remote key (documented for the screen-off feature) |
| Always Ready | `on`, `off` (read from `general/lifeOnScreenMode`; writes only with confirmation) | Always Ready on blocks the mic, or is the only listening state | `select`, **disabled by default** | Stored original value, one button "Restore TV power settings" |
| Quick Start+ | `on`, `off` (`option/quickStartMode`) | It decides between Suspend (frozen) and a cold boot, or breaks wake | `select`, **disabled by default** | Same restore button |
| Auto-off guards (read-only report) | shows `noSignalAutoOff`, `autoOff15Min`, `screenSaverEnabled`, `noOperationAutoOff` | Any of them powers the TV off into Suspend while in mode A | `binary_sensor` "Settings that can stop listening" | Menu path text |
| Current TV power state | `Active`, `Screen Off`, ... (G1) | Always | `text_sensor`, read-only | n/a |

**Behaviour rules**

1. The daemon never writes a TV setting on its own. A write needs an explicit user action in the app (a confirm step that lists the consequences) or an HA entity change on an entity the user enabled. The satellite reads the value first, stores the original value in a persistent file, and offers one action that restores every value it changed to the stored original. It reads the value back after each write and shows a failure instead of retrying.
2. Entity names and states carry only a short consequence ("Quick Start+ off: slower power-on, wake by LAN works"); HA entities cannot show a paragraph. A read-only `text_sensor` "Power advice" gives one line. The full text lives in the app and in the HA documentation of the integration. (ESPHome entity descriptions carry `entity_category` and `disabled_by_default`; confirm against the vendored proto before relying on them.)
3. The app screen for each option shows, next to the control: **power draw** (higher than standby; the measured figure from D5 once it exists, "not measured" until then), **panel and OLED considerations** (screen-off avoids static images but may skip compensation; the background screen lights the panel), **what stops working** (for example: with Quick Start+ on, WoL may be ignored and the satellite freezes in standby; with Always Ready on, LG's voice may take the mic), and **how to revert** (the restore button, then the TV's own menu path: Settings > General > ... for Always Ready and Quick Start+, OLED Care > Screen Off for the screen).
4. Defaults: nothing changed. Options that change LG settings are disabled in HA by default and hidden behind an "Advanced" fold in the app.

## 8. Test plan

The updated, agent-runnable plan is the [D5 plan](../discovery/c3-a/D5-plan.md): a read-only power-state log, a way-back check before any standby, the screen-off, screen saver, Always Ready and Suspend steps, a short quiet playback check in each state with the volume read first and restored exactly, and a long "all day screen-off" soak. ### 10.2 Candidates, ranked

Ranking is by expected robustness for the owner's goal, risk, and effort. Every behavioural claim about c3-a below is a **hypothesis** until [D5](../discovery/c3-a/D5-plan.md#remote-power-off-tests-owner-goal-look-off-keep-listening) runs.

#### 1. Always Ready as it is now (dark variant). Rank 1

- **What it is:** nothing to build. The remote's POWER key already puts c3-a into Always Ready (`alwaysReady` = without wallpaper).
- **Documented:** entered after "you switch it off using the remote control" ([LG webOS 24](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-tv-how-to-use-the-always-ready-function-CT10000018-20153870949064)); "Hi LG" voice recognition works with the background screen off ([webOS 22](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-tv-how-to-use-the-always-ready-feature-webos-22-CT10000018-20153280995321)); sound from smart devices and Sound Palette plays, so the audio path works in that mode; "Energy consumption may increase"; exit with the Magic Remote's power button; "will not turn on if the power cord is removed". LG gives **no timeout, no timers and no OLED guidance** on these pages.

Three sub-routes, from least to most invasive. **Labels:** the method names are verified-on-target as declared; behaviour is hypothesis; the OSE source below is `documented` for OSE only.

What follows for c3-a:

- **The POWER key may not be an input event at all**: in standby the same README says "a button pressed in standby never reaches the input device" (the standby controller wakes with a reason named after the button) and tvpowerd has `remoteKey` and `localKey` reasons. Whether an active-TV IR POWER press produces `KEY_POWER` (116) on `LGE RCU` is **unknown** (D5 passive check, no grab).
- **IR versus Magic Remote:** they are separate event devices on c3-a (`LGE RCU` event1 versus `Smart Remote RCU Input` event9 and `LGE M-RCU - Builtin` nodes); a hook would have to cover each, and BT-remote power-off may be handled by other logic. **Hypothesis**, no source.
- **Not key events, so never caught by any remap:** HDMI-CEC power-off (`hdmiCec` reason), auto-off timers (`autoOff`, `nosignal`, `noMotionAutoOff`, `sleepTimer`, `offTimer`), voice power-off (`voice`, `powerOffByWov`), HA/SSAP turn-off (it is a key send via the network-input service, which a hook on a different device would not see; hypothesis).
- **Risk:** medium. Grabbing an event device can lock the owner out of the remote while the grab is held (it releases when the process closes the fd). A bad hook on `tvservice` is reported to break picture or wake on other models. **Persistence needed** for it to apply after reboot.
- **A rooted-only alternative, our own `evdev` reader** (read-only, no grab) is useful as a **measurement** (D5), not as the product path: it would need the grab to swallow the key.

#### 4. Accept Suspend and wake by WoL. Rank 4 (for completeness; not listening)

- The TV is in `Suspend` after the remote key when Quick Start+ is on and Always Ready is off (observed-on-other-device, section 3). The process is frozen (not killed), the network is not reliably up (`webos-connman-adapter` is told to prepare for suspend on c3-a), and **no listening and no reply happens**. HA's turn-on is WoL, `wakeOnLan` and `wakeOnWiFi` are declared, and the last power-on reason on c3-a was `wakeOnWiFi`.
- It is the right **fallback if rank 1 fails**: the TV looks off, the satellite resumes after any wake (G2), and a deliberate "wake and reply" is an HA automation. Document it as "not always-on" in the UI ([section 7](#7-exposing-power-options-in-the-ui-design)).

### 10.4 Recommendation

1. **Test rank 1 first, because it is already configured and already the owner's real action.** c3-a has the dark variant on. If D5 shows userland, the capture PCM, the network and playback working in that state for hours, the "remote off with the TV looking off" goal is met with no new code, and the supported setup is "Always Ready (without wallpaper) on, Quick Start+ on, Wake on LAN/Wi-Fi on", checked by the keep-awake guard (G6) and shown in the UI (G7). The daemon must **not** stop capture because the power state is not `Active`: decide from the PCM itself and use the power state only for reporting (G1).
2. **If rank 1 fails** (frozen, network down, mic taken, or a timer ends it), run the rank 2 probes in the order 2a, 2c, 2b, each time-boxed, and keep **mode A** (screen off on a running TV, section 4) as the supported state in the meantime.
3. **Do not pursue rank 3 for POWER.** The only documented project says power is deliberately not remappable, and the other is unverified on this firmware. Use evdev only to measure.
4. **Keep rank 4 as the documented fallback**, honestly labelled as "not listening".
5. **Privacy:** a TV that looks off while the mic is open must say so. The product needs a visible status (HA entity, the TV's standby LED setting `standByLight` was on), a one-switch mic mute and the kill switch; document it in the install UX.

### 10.5 What D5 must prove

1. After the **real remote key**, and separately after `controls/power`: the state sequence (`getPowerState`, `alwaysready/aod/getStatus`), whether SSH and the HA link stay up, the satellite pid, the PCM state and frames, for the first minute, at 5 min, and across an hours-long soak.
2. The panel is dark (the owner confirms), and the power draw in each state.
3. Which timer, if any, ends the state (`aod30sec`, `autoOffByAoDTimer`, no-signal, HDMI input behaviour), and the resulting state.
4. No `MIC BUSY`; a quiet tone plays with the volume protocol.
5. `suspend_stats/success` before and after (is it a kernel suspend?).
6. A passive trace: does the IR POWER key appear as an event, on which node (settles rank 3).
7. Whether the TV wakes from every state by WoL, the remote and `controls/power` (the way-back gate).

## 11. Result on c3-b (2026-11-03)

- **Run:** the [D5 plan](../discovery/c3-a/D5-plan.md) on a second LG C3 (c3-b, firmware `03.30.73`), with the aarch64 daemon from `/tmp`. Full tables, timings, deviations and what was restored: [D5 results](../discovery/c3-b/D5-results.md); unit facts: [D0 quick](../discovery/c3-b/D0-quick.md). Everything below is `verified-on-target` on that unit and firmware, in the states named; c3-a is a different unit.
- **State sequence after a power-off** (API key `networkinput/controls/power`, and two real IR-remote presses that a person made, uncontrolled): `Request Power Off` (reason `remoteKey`) then `Active Standby` at +3 to +4 s. With **Always Ready off**: `Request Suspend` (reason `oslbSaving`) at +7 to +15 s, `Suspend`, network down about 10 to 20 s after the key, a kernel suspend (`suspend_stats/success` +1), the process **frozen** (same pid after the wake), the mic PCM `SUSPENDED` then `RUNNING` within about 20 ms of the resume, the HA TCP session kept. With **Always Ready on** (`alwaysReady` and `allEnabled` alike): it **stays in `Active Standby`**, no suspend, network up, process and mic alive, `aod` status `none`.
- **Duration:** 59 min 18 s, ended by a power-on with reason `remoteKey`; no timer ended it (home app, not an HDMI input). Longer stays not seen.
- **Wake:** `controls/power` (a toggle) and a real key wake `Active Standby` in 3 s; WoL woke `Suspend` in 2 to 4 s (with `wolwowlOnOff` set to `true`; the unit's own value was `false`, whose effect is untested). No HA turn-on path exists (no webostv entity).
- **Listening in Always Ready:** no `MIC BUSY` (LG hands-free off), no stalls; six wake word detections ran the whole chain (STT, intent, TTS stream to `pacat`).
- **Replying in Always Ready: fails.** The tone was not heard by the mic in `Active Standby` (five checks, ARC and TV speaker outputs), though PulseAudio and ALSA playback ran. It is heard in `Active`, `Screen Saver` and `Screen Off`. The cause is not isolated (a mute or power-down in the amp or soundbar path, **hypothesis**).
- **Input nodes:** the physical IR POWER key is a `KEY_POWER` event on `LGE RCU`; the API key appears on `Smart Remote RCU Input`, not on `LGE Network Input` (an observation for rank 3; it does not change that rank).
- **Rank 2 probes:** `sleepd` `activityStart` hold (2a): no effect on the suspend. `cancelPowerOff {}` in the window (2c): returned true, no effect. 2b not run. Both negative.
- **Consequences for the design:**
  1. With Always Ready on, the "TV looks off and still listens" half of the goal is met by LG's own setting and needs no new code (on this unit; panel and draw not yet observed).
  2. The "play a reply" half is not met in that state. A reply needs the TV out of `Active Standby`. **Untested idea (hypothesis):** on a wake word in `Active Standby`, the satellite wakes the TV with `controls/power` (3 s to `Active`), then calls `turnOffScreen` (`Screen Off` plays sound). This lights the panel briefly and adds about 3 s before the first sound; it needs its own test, with people in the room warned.
  3. Mode A (`turnOffScreen` on a running TV) stays the supported state where replies must be heard.
  4. The sound output can revert to ARC after a power cycle, so a feature that needs `tv_speaker` must set it each time and read it back.
  5. G6 (keep-awake guard) needs to read `lifeOnScreenMode`, `wolwowlOnOff` and `quickStartMode` and say what each does; `suspend_stats/success` and the power-state log are cheap probes for the satellite itself.
- **Open:** the long soak with a real remote, the Magic Remote, panel and draw, why `Active Standby` is silent, WoL from `Active Standby` and with `wolwowlOnOff = false`, the wake-and-darken idea. These need a quiet window agreed with the owner; the room is in use.
