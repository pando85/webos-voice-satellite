# Always-on design: listening and replying while the TV screen is off

- **Status:** **proposal, nothing implemented.** Desk research plus read-only inspection of c3-a on 2026-11-03. No power state was changed, no setting was written, no sound was played. The test procedure that would settle the open points is [D5 plan](../discovery/c3-a/D5-plan.md) (agent-runnable, **not run yet**).
- **Date:** 2026-11-03. Device in scope for TV facts: c3-a (LG C3, webOS TV 8.3.0, firmware 03.30.74, rooted with the Homebrew Channel). Other models and firmware are not covered; a finding from another LG model is not confirmation on this one.
- **Owner question (2026-11-03):** can the satellite run all the time, listen with the TV screen off and still work, and can it play sound with the screen off?
- **Related:** [autostart design](autostart.md), [install and configuration UX](install-and-config-ux.md), [EXP-004](../experiments/EXP-004-power-lifecycle.md), [P3 robustness](../discovery/c3-a/P3-robustness.md), [D2 audio stack](../discovery/c3-a/D2-audio-stack.md), [roadmap Phase 3](../project/roadmap.md#phase-3-lifecycle-and-robustness).

Labels follow [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md): `documented` (vendor or upstream text), `observed-on-other-device` (reports or code for other LG TVs), `hypothesis`, `verified-on-target` (seen on c3-a). "Verified-on-target" below means a read-only observation of the device as it is now (TV on, screen on); it never means a power state was entered.

## 1. Short answer

| Question | Answer today |
| --- | --- |
| Can it run all the time? | **Yes while the TV is on, including with the screen off** (the process, the capture PCM and the HA connection do not depend on the panel; hypothesis until D5). **No while the TV is in standby (Suspend)**: the process is frozen, not killed, and the network is not reliably up (observed-on-other-device). A TV powered off with Quick Start+ off is a cold boot and everything restarts. |
| How to listen with the screen off? | Use **"screen off" on a running TV** (luna `tvpower/power/turnOffScreen`, the HA webostv "Screen" switch, or the OLED Care "Screen Off" menu), not the remote's power button. This is the recommended mode ([section 4](#4-what-always-on-realistically-means-and-recommended-modes)). |
| Can it play sound with the screen off? | **Likely yes** in that mode: the feature exists so that the TV "keeps running, so you can keep listening to the sound" (documented, HA) and users play audio through it (observed-on-other-device). Not verified on c3-a with our `pacat` path; that is a D5 step. In standby there is no playback for our process (frozen). |

The webOS OSE `sleepd` documents activity-based wakeups ([OSE API, OSE only](https://webosose.org/docs/reference/ls2-api/com-webos-service-sleep)); that API is not exposed on this TV as far as the permission files show, and OSE is not proof for commercial webOS.

## 4. What "always on" realistically means and recommended modes

Three facts bound the design:

1. **Our process is frozen in Suspend** and the capture PCM and network are not ours to keep (observed-on-other-device). Nothing in the repository or in public reports gives a way to keep a rooted daemon running through LG's standby. The only LG feature that listens with the TV "off" is LG's own wake-on-voice, which runs in LG's firmware and ends in LG's assistant. We do not claim a way to reuse it.
2. **A TV that is "on" with the panel dark behaves like a running computer** (documented for the screen-off feature). That is the state in which the satellite can listen and speak.
3. **Auto-off timers can undo it.** On c3-a `noSignalAutoOff` and `autoOff15Min` are on (15 minutes without signal turns the TV off), `screenSaverEnabled` is on. If the screen-off TV sits on an HDMI input with no signal, the TV may power itself off into Suspend and freeze the satellite (**hypothesis**; the effect of the timers while `Screen Off` is not known).

**Recommended operating modes, in order**

| Mode | What the user does | Satellite | Trade-offs | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Screen off (recommended)** | Leaves the TV on and turns the screen off with HA (webostv "Screen" switch), the remote shortcut or a future satellite entity; stays on a real app or an input with signal | Runs, listens, replies; HA connection stays up | Panel power saved; SoC, audio and Wi-Fi stay on, so draw is above standby (**no figure measured**, plug meter planned). OLED: the panel is off, which avoids static-image wear, but OLED compensation and pixel refresh runs after power-off (`compensationStart`/`End` reasons exist, [lg-webos-dashboard PR 150](https://github.com/rorygallagher2024/lg-webos-dashboard/pull/150)); a TV that is never "off" may skip it (**hypothesis**). The TV's `Screen Off` may end on a remote key press or an auto-off timer | hypothesis until D5 steps 1, 3 |
| **B. Always Ready on** | Uses the remote power key; the TV shows its background screen | Probably runs (OS awake for the app) and keeps the HA link; may lose the mic to LG's voice daemon (MIC BUSY) | LG warns consumption rises; the background screen lights the panel (burn-in is LG's concern here, screen-off timer `screenOffTime`); `Hi LG` and ours would compete for the PCM | unknown, D5 steps 4 to 5 |
| **C. Standby with wake on demand** | Remote power key; HA turns the TV on by WoL when needed (webostv turn-on), then optionally screen-off | Frozen while in standby, runs again after wake (reconnect, PCM reopen) | Lowest draw; **no voice assistant while in standby**; WoL may not work with Quick Start+ on | works as "off", not as "always on" |
| **D. Cold off, boot on demand** | Quick Start+ off, Always Ready off | Starts from `init.d` at each power-on (60 s delay in the draft hook) | Slow wake (a boot); needs the persistent autostart | fallback |

**Can the satellite request screen-on or a wake when a reply needs it?** For voice replies: no need, audio does not need the panel (hypothesis for mode A, D5 step 3). If a visual or a "wake the TV" feature is wanted later: with the TV running, `turnOnScreen` over luna (declared on c3-a; the HA "Screen" switch uses the same method over the network, documented); from standby the satellite cannot (it is frozen), HA can (WoL, `wakeOnLan`/`wakeOnWiFi` declared). Note that HA's turn-on is itself WoL-based on webOS, so it shares the Wi-Fi/Quick Start+ caveats above.

**Power-draw trade-off.** No measured figure exists for c3-a in any state. LG states that energy consumption "may increase" in Always Ready and in standby-mode features (documented). Mode A should draw less than screen-on and more than Suspend (hypothesis). The D5 plan asks for a plug-meter reading in each state (owner's plug or the homelab power metering if one exists) before this document recommends anything beyond "try it".

## 5. Playback with the screen off: known and to test

- **Known:** the replies reach PulseAudio and the TV speaker with the screen on and in the `ScreenSaver` state (verified-on-target, [P1](../discovery/c3-a/P1-playback-silent-validation.md), [P2 session 1](../discovery/c3-a/P2-session1-audible-and-echo.md)). The screen-off feature is documented to leave sound playing.
- **Not known on c3-a:** whether our `pacat` stream is heard in `Screen Off` (an A/V mute service, `avmutemanager`, runs on the TV and its trigger rules are unknown), whether the master volume and mute are unchanged by the transition, whether the amp/DSP path stays powered, and whether the TV levels the sound the same way. Also unknown: whether playback works in Always Ready, and the latency of the first reply after a long idle period with the screen off.
- **Must be tested (D5):** a short, quiet reply-equivalent tone in each state that is safe to enter, with the TV volume read first and restored to the exact original value ([D5 plan](../discovery/c3-a/D5-plan.md#playback-check-short-and-quiet)).

## 7. Exposing power options in the UI (design)

**Rule (owner, 2026-11-03):** if D5 shows that a power-related setting is **critical** for the satellite to work, it must be exposed as a configuration option, both as an **HA entity on the device page** and in the **install and status app** planned in [install and configuration UX](install-and-config-ux.md) (the web app in the HBC package, section 4.1). Each option carries its risks next to it. Design only; nothing here is implemented, and which options are critical is decided by the D5 evidence.

**Candidate options** (each is added only if D5 shows it matters):

| Option | Values | Becomes critical if | HA entity (config category, one per option) | Revert |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TV screen policy | `leave alone`, `screen off when idle (N min)`, `screen off now` | Mode A is the only state where listening and replies work | `select` plus a `switch` "Screen off" mirroring the real state | The switch, or any remote key (documented for the screen-off feature) |
| Always Ready | `on`, `off` (read from `general/lifeOnScreenMode`; writes only with confirmation) | Always Ready on blocks the mic, or is the only listening state | `select`, **disabled by default** | Stored original value, one button "Restore TV power settings" |
| Quick Start+ | `on`, `off` (`option/quickStartMode`) | It decides between Suspend (frozen) and a cold boot, or breaks wake | `select`, **disabled by default** | Same restore button |
| Auto-off guards (read-only report) | shows `noSignalAutoOff`, `autoOff15Min`, `screenSaverEnabled`, `noOperationAutoOff` | Any of them powers the TV off into Suspend while in mode A | `binary_sensor` "Settings that can stop listening" | Menu path text |
| Current TV power state | `Active`, `Screen Off`, ... (G1) | Always | `text_sensor`, read-only | n/a |

**Behaviour rules**

1. The daemon never writes a TV setting on its own. A write needs an explicit user action in the app (a confirm step that lists the consequences) or an HA entity change on an entity the user enabled. The satellite reads the value first, stores the original value in a persistent file, and offers one action that restores every value it changed to the stored original. It reads the value back after each write and shows a failure instead of retrying.
2. Entity names and states carry only a short consequence ("Quick Start+ off: slower power-on, wake by LAN works"); HA entities cannot show a paragraph. A read-only `text_sensor` "Power advice" gives one line. The full text lives in the app and in the HA documentation of the integration. (ESPHome entity descriptions carry `entity_category` and `disabled_by_default`; confirm against the vendored proto before relying on them.)
3. The app screen for each option shows, next to the control: **power draw** (higher than standby; the measured figure from D5 once it exists, "not measured" until then), **panel and OLED considerations** (screen-off avoids static images but may skip compensation; the background screen lights the panel), **what stops working** (for example: with Quick Start+ on, WoL may be ignored and the satellite freezes in standby; with Always Ready on, LG's voice may take the mic), and **how to revert** (the restore button, then the TV's own menu path: Settings > General > ... for Always Ready and Quick Start+, OLED Care > Screen Off for the screen).
4. Defaults: nothing changed. Options that change LG settings are disabled in HA by default and hidden behind an "Advanced" fold in the app.

## 8. Test plan

The updated, agent-runnable plan is the [D5 plan](../discovery/c3-a/D5-plan.md): a read-only power-state log, a way-back check before any standby, the screen-off, screen saver, Always Ready and Suspend steps, a short quiet playback check in each state with the volume read first and restored exactly, and a long "all day screen-off" soak. The owner allowed agents to change power states on 2026-11-03; the orchestrator schedules the run because the TV is in daily use. The owner also allowed TV settings changes and reboots when a test needs them (2026-11-03), with every changed setting read first and restored, no firmware writes, LG hands-free off unless a test needs it, and `/tmp` re-installed after a reboot.

## 9. Open questions (top three need the D5 session)

1. **Does the satellite keep listening, and do `pacat` replies sound, in `Screen Off`?** And how long does `Screen Off` last before a timer or screen saver ends it?
2. **What exactly do the remote power key and an API power-off do on c3-a** (Quick Start+ and Always Ready are both on): Always Ready with the OS running, `Active Standby` then `Suspend`, or something else; is the satellite frozen, and is the network up in each?
3. **Can the TV be woken without the remote** from that state (WoL to the Wi-Fi or the wired MAC, HA turn-on)? This is also the safety gate for every standby step.
4. Whether LG's voice daemon takes the capture PCM in Always Ready (MIC BUSY), and whether resume leaves the PCM and TCP socket usable.
5. Power draw in each state (no number exists).
