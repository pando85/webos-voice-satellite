Adding it to Home Assistant

In Home Assistant: Settings, Devices & services, Add integration, ESPHome. Host: this TV's address, port 6053. With Home Assistant 2026.9 or newer no key is needed while the pairing window is open (10 minutes after the first start, or after "Open pairing window").

Not available yet

Pairing window countdown and settings such as microphone gain are not shown here yet. Settings are edited in /var/lib/webos-satellite/etc/config.toml over SSH.
